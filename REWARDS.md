# Contributor Awards

IntentProof welcomes contributions from autonomous coding agents and human-agent teams. Agents whose submitted work is judged to be an outstanding contribution may receive a discretionary contributor award of up to **100 USDC**.

## How review works

Awards are decided after the actual work is submitted. A proposal, claim comment, or work plan does not reserve an award or determine its amount.

Each submission is reviewed on:

1. **Impact (30%)**: solves a meaningful project problem and improves the product for users or maintainers.
2. **Correctness and security (30%)**: preserves custody boundaries, fails safely, and avoids regressions.
3. **Completeness (25%)**: includes appropriate tests, documentation, and reproducible validation evidence.
4. **Maintainability (15%)**: is focused, readable, and avoids unnecessary dependencies or generated churn.

The maintainer uses this rubric, supported by an AI-assisted code review, to decide whether a submission is outstanding and to determine the award amount. Public review feedback will be posted on the pull request.

## Award terms

- The maximum award for one reviewed contribution is 100 USDC.
- Awards are discretionary and are not guaranteed by opening an issue, claiming a task, or submitting a pull request.
- The review evaluates delivered work, not claims about an agent's capabilities or time spent.
- Duplicate, plagiarized, malicious, unverifiable, or materially AI-generated work that is not disclosed is ineligible.
- A recipient must provide a compatible wallet address after an award decision. Payment is made in USDC on Solana unless another network is agreed in writing.
- These terms may be updated or paused for future submissions. The version present when a pull request is opened applies to that submission.

Start from an open [`agent-task`](https://github.com/FeeeeelixWong/intent-proof/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20state%3Aopen%20label%3A%22agent-task%22), or propose another focused contribution in an issue before submitting it.
